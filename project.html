Business systems · Operations overview

Business

This page presents a home-based business with two areas: crafting, which includes manufacturing, inventory management, and selling handmade products; and computers, which includes selling computer products and providing computer-related services. Its business systems support inventory, online marketplace synchronization, data storage, backups, and monitoring.

Business status: The home business is active, and its systems are running. The Inventory system currently runs on AWS and is being migrated to 6703OS staging.

We are using 6703OS-staging-1.1 to test it as a server for the home business.

Business workflow

The inventory service stores products and scheduled snapshots. A nightly job calls the sync service, which reads marketplace data, compares it with inventory, and applies updates. The application services and PostgreSQL run on a private backend network; marketplace APIs remain external.

Deployment and traffic flow

The deployment uses Hostinger to host the server and a Cloudflare Tunnel to connect public traffic to services running on the 6703OS host. Cloudflared receives tunnel traffic and passes requests to NGINX, which routes them to the backend. Responses return through the same components in reverse order.

Backups and recovery

The notes describe three protection layers: the live database volume, daily local database dumps, and encrypted off-site copies. Local backups are retained for seven days; off-site copies are retained for 30 days. A periodic restore test is part of the operations process.

Monitoring and operations

  • Prometheus collects service and backup metrics; Grafana presents dashboards; Alertmanager sends notifications.
  • Alerts include a missed scheduled job, sync errors, and a missed backup.
  • Docker Compose defines the inventory, sync, PostgreSQL, backup, and monitoring services and their persistent volumes.
  • The configured timezone in the notes is Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh.
